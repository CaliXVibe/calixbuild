# Privacy Policy

**CaliX.Build** · Last updated August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CaliX.Build collects, uses, and shares information when you use our websites, widgets, chat, forms, and related services.

## Information we collect

- Name, company, email, phone, and messages you send through our forms, chat, and widgets
- Usage and device logs for reliability and security
- Billing details needed to run subscriptions

## How we use it

- Answer you, send quotes, and run subscriptions
- Keep the site secure and prevent abuse
- Improve the product

## Sharing

We share data only with processors that run the product — hosting, SMS, email, and CRM tools like Jobber when you connect one. **We do not sell personal information.**

## California rights

California residents may opt out of any sale or sharing by emailing hello@calixbuild.com with the subject **Do Not Sell or Share**. We honor Global Privacy Control signals. You can request access or deletion at the same address.
